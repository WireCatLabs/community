# Code of conduct

WireCat welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Treat people respectfully in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other spaces managed by project maintainers.

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; do not insult or harass people.
- Do not discriminate, threaten, or use sexual harassment or unwanted sexual content.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information or messages
  without permission.
- Keep discussion relevant and avoid spam or repeated disruption.
- Follow moderator instructions and give others room to participate.

Report a concern privately to [hello@wirecat.dev](mailto:hello@wirecat.dev).
Include links and context where safe, without exposing unrelated private data.
Maintainers will limit sharing of reports to what is needed to assess and respond;
absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request a change in behavior, or restrict
participation temporarily or permanently according to the severity and context.
They are expected to apply these standards to themselves as well as contributors.
If a decision appears mistaken, contact the same address with additional context.

This policy also applies when someone represents a WireCat project in public.
