projects and third-party files are governed by their own license notices.

- [Maintainer checklist](MAINTAINERS.md): ownership, third-party attribution,
  distribution obligations, branding, and release controls.
- [Contribution licensing and DCO](../contributing/README.md#license-and-your-right-to-contribute):

Adding policies does not complete a historical provenance audit, register a
trademark, or establish a separate contributor license agreement.
